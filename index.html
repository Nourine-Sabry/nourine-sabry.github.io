about

I'm a bioinformatician and Teaching Assistant at Nile University, where I teach Omics data analysis, computer-aided drug discovery, and algorithmic bioinformatics. I graduated summa cum laude with a B.Sc. in Biotechnology, specializing in Bioinformatics, from Nile University in 2025.

My work sits at the intersection of systems biology and structural bioinformatics — building and mining biological networks to surface promising drug targets, then following them through docking and virtual screening. I like projects that make me work from raw, messy data instead of pre-cleaned examples; that's usually where the real debugging (and learning) happens.
